### Outputs

**O1 — Verdict:** 🛑 **Don't borrow**
> A recent payment bounce combined with existing high-cost debt means a new loan will likely make things worse, not better.
> Flags: `recent_bounce`, `high_cost_debt`

**O2, O3, O4 — not shown**

Per the app's design, once the verdict is "don't borrow," the amount/rate/EMI numbers are suppressed rather than displayed as misleading figures. Internally, the raw calculation shows a safe-carry figure of ₹0 (she is already over her adjusted safe FOIR ceiling before any new loan is added, given her existing obligations and two dependents) — this is correct math, but the UI intentionally does not surface it as a headline number, since a literal "₹0" reads as a bug rather than a deliberate stop signal.

### Negotiation Card summary
- **Headline:** Not a rate-negotiation card in this case. The card instead states plainly why a new loan isn't advisable right now, so Anita has something to show a lender, family member, or the app-loan providers if needed.
- **Content shown:** the verdict reason and flags only — no amount/rate/EMI figures.
